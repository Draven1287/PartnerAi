Featured · Aarav Shah · Founder of LearningAI

A small LLM, trained on a MacBook Air

In plain language: Aarav started with a small existing AI model and practiced one narrow task with it—turning ordinary questions into shorter, SQL-formatted responses. He compared the model before and after so the change was visible.

Built with: Llama 3.2 1B · Hugging Face dataset · LoRA fine-tuning · MacBook Air M3

What it means: a teenager can investigate how model behavior changes using an ordinary laptop, careful evidence, and a narrow goal.

Before training

The prompt “Show me all students in 10th grade” produced a chatty 72-token markdown explanation.

After training

The same prompt produced a shorter 39-token SQL response intended to be ready for a database.

Recorded result summary—not the raw model transcript. This one comparison does not prove general SQL accuracy; every important query still needs testing.

How publication works

You stay in control of your work.

  1. Prepare. Include the goal, your decisions, evidence, one mistake, and what you learned. Remove faces, location data, school details, and private information.
  2. Email for review. Sending a project is only a request for review. It does not give LearningAI permission to publish it.
  3. Approve separately. If the project is selected, LearningAI asks again what may appear. Learners under 18 must use a non-identifying pseudonym and need guardian approval of the exact preview.

You keep ownership. Your email address is never shown in the Gallery. Learners under 18 may appear only under a non-identifying pseudonym—not a real name, username, handle, school, team, employer, city, exact age, birthday, face, voice, or contact detail. A guardian cannot approve those identifiers for publication. No under-18 project may be published until LearningAI receives and privately records a separate guardian approval for the exact publication preview. Until that process is complete, the work remains unpublished. You may ask to change or remove a published entry at any time. Selection is based on clear evidence and a useful learning story—not grades, popularity, or polish. Read the full submission and deletion policy →
