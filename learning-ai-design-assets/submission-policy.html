Project submission and deletion policy

Submitting a project — through the site or by email — is a request for private review. It is never automatic permission to publish.

How a submission reaches LearningAI

There are two routes, and they are handled the same way once received.

  • Through the site. Submit a project records your work on your LearningAI account and places it in the private review queue. The address used is the one you signed in with; the form never asks you to type an address and never sends to anyone else.
  • By email. The Gallery contact page prepares a message that you send yourself from your own email app.

LearningAI does not send email on your behalf. Outbound email is not switched on for this site. A submission made through the site is recorded, not emailed, and no page will tell you an email was sent.

What to leave out of a submission

A submission is read by a person, so keep it free of anything that identifies you or someone else. Before you send, take out:

  • Real names — yours and anyone else’s — and usernames, handles, or nicknames that point to a real person.
  • School, class, team, club, or employer details.
  • Home or street addresses, and the city or town you live in.
  • Your exact age or birthday.
  • Faces and voices, in images, video, or audio.
  • Contact details of any kind: email addresses, phone numbers, profile links.
  • Identity documents, school records, health information, and passwords or keys.
  • Anything private that belongs to another person, including private messages and screenshots of them.

If your project genuinely needs one of these to make sense, describe it instead of showing it — for example, “a message from a classmate” rather than the message.

Where replies go

A reply goes to the email address on the LearningAI account you signed in with, and nowhere else. The submission form shows that address but never lets you type a different one, so the site cannot be used to write to a stranger. To change the address a reply would reach, change the account you sign in with, or update the account address in Settings.

Your address is never shown in the Gallery and is never published.

What happens after a submission

  1. Aarav reviews the project privately for safety, clarity, ownership, and useful evidence. Review is manual and no response time is promised.
  2. If it is not selected, it remains unpublished. The submission, its recorded form answers, and any email and attachments are deleted within 30 days after the review decision.
  3. If it may be selected, LearningAI creates an exact publication preview and asks for a separate written approval.

Learners under 18

Under-18 work stays unpublished until a parent or guardian approves the exact preview in a separate message. LearningAI privately records the approval date, approved non-identifying pseudonym, approved files, and the guardian’s confirmation. A guardian cannot approve a real name or other personal identifier for publication. If the confirmation is missing or unclear, publication stops.

What appears publicly

Only the approved project story, approved evidence, and approved public name may appear. A learner under 18 must use a non-identifying pseudonym. Real names, usernames or handles, schools, teams, employers, cities or locations, exact ages or birthdays, faces or voices, contact details, identity documents, private messages, third-party information, and metadata are excluded. Learners keep ownership of their work.

Retention and deletion

  • A submission made through the site is stored against your LearningAI account. Deleting your account in Settings deletes it with everything else on that account.
  • Rejected submissions and their attachments are deleted within 30 days after the rejection decision.
  • Withdrawn submissions and their attachments are deleted within 30 days after the withdrawal request.
  • If required information remains missing, LearningAI gives the submitter a closure date. The incomplete submission and its attachments are deleted within 30 days after that closure date.
  • Published entries retain only the minimum approval record while the entry is public.
  • A verified removal request unpublishes the entry and deletes both the private review material and minimum approval record within 30 days after identity is confirmed, unless a legal requirement requires a longer period.
  • LearningAI does not store submission data in the public website repository.

Contact and identity checks

Use learningai4youprojects@duck.com to withdraw, correct, or delete a submission. A deletion or correction request may require a reply from the original submitting address or guardian address. Do not send passwords, identity documents, school records, or health information.

Current status: review is manual. No project is published automatically, and no response-time guarantee is offered.
