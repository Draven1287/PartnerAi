Featured project · by Aarav

I built a small LLM on my MacBook Air

15 years old. 1 laptop. About 4 minutes of training. No additional cloud-compute spend.

The idea was not to build a frontier model from scratch. Aarav borrowed Meta's free Llama 3.2 1B Instruct model and fine-tuned it toward one narrow text-to-SQL task.

Llama 3.2 1BBase model
2,000SQL examples
0.228%Parameters trained
~4 minTraining time
3.18 → 1.18Training loss
$0Additional cloud-compute spend

Recipe

  • Base model: Llama 3.2 1B Instruct, 4-bit quantized.
  • Dataset: 2,000 SQL examples from Hugging Face, b-mc2/sql-create-context.
  • Method: LoRA fine-tuning with Apple MLX on a MacBook Air M3 with 16 GB RAM.
  • Run: 300 iterations and 1.44 GB peak memory.

Recorded result

For one recorded prompt—“Show me all students in 10th grade”—the pre-training response was a chatty 72-token markdown explanation. The post-training response was a shorter 39-token SQL response intended for a database workflow.

Limit: this is a summary of one local run, not the raw transcript or proof of general SQL accuracy. Important queries still require testing and human review.

Case study

Multi-agent ethics dialogue experiment

Made by Aarav, Felix, and AI.

Two AI systems were tested as strategic negotiators and ethical decision-makers across trolley problems, prisoner’s dilemma cases, treaty negotiation, and war-negotiation scenarios.

  • Current priority: the Treaty of Westmark, with General Mara Vance negotiating against General Darian Voss.
  • The setup tests asymmetric private information, deception/manipulation pressure, repeated diplomacy loops, and whether a settlement is reached.
  • The runs save JSON transcripts, checkpoints, terminal logs, final treaty drafts, private/public responses, secretary interventions, and historian analysis.
  • The analysis scripts turn treaty, prisoner, trolley, and negotiation outputs into CSV summaries and presentation dashboard views.

Next step: turn the experiment files into a readable public case study with screenshots, example JSON snippets, charts, and reflection questions.
