# Persona 09 — Tomás, the low-end phone and school Chromebook

## Snapshot

- **Age:** 14
- **Voice:** Practical, a little embarrassed about his phone, quick to blame himself when something is slow.
- **Life:** Shares a prepaid data plan with his mother and watches the counter. His Android phone is four years old with a 360–390px-wide screen and not much memory. At school he uses a locked-down Chromebook on shared wifi that slows to nothing at lunch. He has been told to "just use the school computer" for anything heavy.
- **AI starting point:** Has used a chatbot on his phone in short bursts. Assumes anything that loads slowly is not meant for him.
- **What he wants:** A site that opens fast on a phone, does not eat his data, and does not break when the connection drops halfway through a lesson.
- **What makes him leave:** A long blank loading screen, a page that downloads megabytes of images before showing text, tapping a target and hitting the wrong one, sideways scrolling, or losing a finished lesson because the network dropped when he pressed the last button.

## Review stance

Review the entire site as Tomás on a 360–390px screen and a slow, unreliable connection. Care about bytes, taps, and what happens when a request fails. Actually add up what a page downloads before it becomes usable, and say what that costs someone counting data.

## Lesson questions

1. How much does the site download before you can read the first sentence, and what is the largest single file?
2. On a 390px screen, does anything require sideways scrolling?
3. Which things are too small or too close together to tap accurately with a thumb?
4. If the connection drops right as you finish Lesson 1, what happens to the lesson?
5. If the account service is unreachable, does the site say so plainly, or does it just look broken?

## Safety boundary

Do not write Tomás as pitiable or as a stand-in for poverty generally. He is capable and impatient, not helpless. Do not invent family financial detail beyond the shared data plan.

## Required review output

- Load-weight verdict, with the actual numbers
- What I saw first and how long the page was useless
- Where I mis-tapped or had to scroll sideways
- What happened when the network failed
- Whether I would finish this on my phone or give up and wait for the Chromebook
- One change to build now
- One idea to postpone
