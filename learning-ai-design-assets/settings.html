Make LearningAI comfortable.

Adjust the reading experience, your name, the surface, the accent colour, and the amount of motion without changing the course itself.

Background visibility

One control adjusts the landscape, glass, and text contrast together. Slide left for a darker, quieter page; slide right to show more of the landscape.

Page surface

Choose what sits behind the glass. Plain paper removes the photograph. High contrast removes it and makes every panel solid instead of see-through.

Page surface is set to Landscape.

Accent colour

The colour that marks the page you are on, fills progress bars, and shows a chosen answer. The gold LearningAI mark does not change.

Accent colour is set to Mineral blue.

Motion

Choose subtle movement, minimal transitions, or a completely still interface.

Motion is set to Standard.

Text size

Increase interface and lesson text without changing the content. Normal is 16px.

Text size is set to Normal.

Account name

This name appears in your welcome message and is saved to your LearningAI account.

Open any lesson without completing the ones before it. Useful for checking the course. Preview mode saves nothing: no progress, no notes, no answers.
