What happens after you send

  1. Aarav reads the project privately for safety, clarity, ownership, and useful evidence. Review is manual and no response time is promised.
  2. If it is not selected, it stays unpublished and the submission is deleted within 30 days of the decision.
  3. If it may be selected, LearningAI builds the exact publication preview and asks for a separate written approval. Under-18 work also needs a guardian’s approval of that preview.

You can ask for a submission to be withdrawn or deleted at any time by writing to learningai4youprojects@duck.com. Read the full submission and deletion policy.
