    Delete an account permanently

    Deleting removes the account itself. It cannot be undone, and LearningAI cannot restore it for you afterwards. You can only delete the account you are signed in to — switch to it first if it is a different one.

    Deleted from LearningAI

    • Your account: email address, password and display name
    • Your six starting-question answers and recorded level
    • Lesson progress, completed lessons and quiz answers
    • Saved Notes and project submissions
    • Recorded focus minutes and page visits
    • This browser’s copy of all of the above

    Not deleted

    • Any other account this browser has used, and its data
    • Email you have already received from LearningAI
    • Database backups, until they age out on their own schedule
    • Hosting-level server logs, which record requests but not your lesson answers
    • Anything you exported or downloaded yourself
