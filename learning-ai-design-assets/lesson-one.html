First Contact · Free lesson

Use AI safely once.

An AI app can write a message for you. It can compare two plans. It can explain something confusing. It can also be wrong and still sound completely sure. So the real question is not whether you use it. It is whether you still decide what happens next.

Decision lab · 4 choices

What would you do next?

There is no right kind of person here. Pick what you would really do, not what sounds good. What you pick decides which reminder you get at the end.

Decision 1 of 4

1. A group chat says school is closed tomorrow.

The picture of the notice looks real. Nobody has said where it came from.

2. You want help writing a reply after an argument.

The part that would help is in a private message. It has real names in it.

3. The reply is smooth, but it does not sound like you.

It says what you mean. You would never use those words.

4. AI picks a cheap place for your group to meet.

The plan only works if today’s price, the opening time, and who is allowed in are all right.

See what is actually happening

AI is not one thing. You are the last part.

“AI” sounds like one clever box. It is really a few parts, working one after another. The words on your screen come from the middle of that chain. They are not the end of it.

1. YouYou start it. You have a question or a problem.
2. The appThe screen you type into. A website, a chat box, a keyboard.
3. The guesserIt has read a huge amount of writing. It guesses which words fit next. People call it a model.
4. HelpersExtras the app can call on: web search, a calculator, your files. People call them tools.
5. You againYou read it, check it if it matters, and decide. That part never moves.
Control rule:An answer can sound completely sure and still be wrong. AI can help. What happens next is still yours.

Watch the guess change

Who you are writing to changes the answer.

The job stays the same: tell someone you cannot come. Change who you are telling. Watch the words move.

A likely answer to a close friend
“Hey, I’m sorry—I can’t make it tonight. Can we find another time?”

Nothing about you changed. Only the person you are writing to. The guesser picks the words that fit that pattern best. Fitting the pattern is not the same as being true. It is not the same as being the right thing to send, either.

Checking challenge · required

Sounding sure is not the same as being right.

An assistant tells you: “The swimming pool closes at 8 p.m. tonight.” Your friend will travel right across town because of what you say next.

Then: what is the best thing to do?
