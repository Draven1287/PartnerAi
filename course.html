The course

Five chapters. Read them in order.

You can finish the first two in an evening. The rest you'll come back to as you start really using AI.

CHAPTER 01 · ~15 min

What AI actually is

How LLMs work, why they hallucinate, the curve nobody warns you about. Start here if you've never thought about how AI is built.
CHAPTER 02 · ~20 min

How to talk to AI

Prompting as a real skill. Bad prompts vs. great ones, side by side. The four ingredients.
CHAPTER 03 · ~20 min · outline

Being a great AI partner

Push back. Don't accept the first answer. Use it to teach you, not to replace you. The cognitive-offloading research, said plainly.
CHAPTER 04 · ~15 min · outline

Tips & tricks

The pro moves. Roles. Examples. Iteration. Long projects. Choosing the right model for the job.
CHAPTER 05 · outline

Build with AI

Five real projects, from a study tutor to training your own small language model. The "make something" track.

How to use this course

Read in order. Each chapter builds on the last. Chapter 1 makes Chapter 2 make sense; Chapter 2 is the foundation of everything else.

Try things as you go. Every chapter has prompts you can run in the free open-source chat in the bottom-right corner of every page. Reading without practising is the slow way to learn this stuff.

Don't rush. 90 minutes of reading. Years of practice.
