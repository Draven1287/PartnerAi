V2 core lesson 01 · agency

Why AI matters, and why you stay in charge.

AI is powerful, but the point is not to let it run your life. The skill is learning where it helps, where it misleads, and what decisions stay yours.

Try first

Someone you know got called out for using AI.

In one setting, AI is allowed for brainstorming and practice, but not for final answers. One person says learning is pointless now. Another says AI is always cheating. What is the least-bad answer?

Never use AI for school.

Too extreme. It protects you from cheating, but it also blocks useful learning tools like hints, quizzes, feedback, and source checks.

Use AI for everything because everyone else will.

Fast, but weak. If AI does the thinking before you build the skill, you become dependent on it.

Use AI when it helps you learn, and keep proof of your thinking.

Best. AI can help you practice, test, plan, and revise. You still own the goal, the proof, and the final decision.

The main idea

AI is a new kind of tool. It can explain, draft, summarize, code, plan, brainstorm, and critique. That does not mean it should replace your thinking.

The real skill is agency: you choose the goal, you check the answer, you decide what matters, and you take responsibility for the final work.

Agency check

AI can give options. You choose goals, values, and final decisions.

Trap to catch

Sounds responsible, but too simple

"If AI helped, then the work is not really yours."

Better thinking

"It depends what AI did. Did it help me learn, or did it replace the thinking I was supposed to do?"
