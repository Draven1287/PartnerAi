V2 core lesson 05 · prompt repair

Prompt repair: goal, context, constraints, format.

A good prompt is not magic wording. It is a better task instruction. You tell the AI what you want, what it needs to know, and how to respond.

Repair station

Three weak prompts. One repair pattern.

Help me prepare.

Add topic, deadline, what you know, and "quiz me before giving answers."

Make my story better.

Add audience, tone, what kind of feedback you want, and what you do not want changed.

Fix my code.

Add the error, what you expected, what you tried, and "give hints before the full fix."

The repair pattern

01

Goal

What are you trying to accomplish?

02

Context

What does the AI need to know?

03

Constraints

What should it avoid or respect?

04

Format

What shape should the answer take?

Trap to catch

Detailed but wrong job

Write the final answer for me in my voice
and make it sound like I did it.

Better job

Ask me questions first. Give hints.
Check my draft. Do not write the final
answer before I try.

Mastery check

Take one weak prompt you would actually use. Add goal, context, constraints, and format. Then explain which part changed the output most.
