V2 core lesson 04 · language models

What an LLM is, without the magic.

An LLM can sound smart because it is good at language, not because every sentence is checked against reality.

Next-token lab

Context changes what comes next.

"The student opened the ___"

Possible: book, laptop, door, test. Without context, several answers fit.

"The student opened the ___ to fix the Python error."

Now laptop or editor is more likely. Context steers the next words.

"The student opened the ___ and found a fake citation."

Now article, source, or page may fit. Language skill still does not prove truth.

The main idea

LLM means large language model. It works with text chunks called tokens. It uses the current context to generate likely next tokens, one after another.

That is why it can write, explain, translate, summarize, and code. It is also why it can sound fluent while being wrong.

Trap to repair

Bad explanation

"LLMs know facts because they read the internet."

Better explanation

"LLMs generate fact-like text from patterns. Important facts need sources, tools, or verification."
