Privacy

What this site knows about you.

The honest version, in plain language. Short answer: very little, and none of it is for sale.

The promise

We will not sell your information. We will not send ads. We will not ask you for money. That's the deal, and everything below is just the detail.

What stays in your browser (and never leaves)

Most of what you do here is saved with localStorage — a small storage area inside your own browser, on your own device. We can't see it. It includes:

  • Your assessment result (your level and answers from "Find your AI style").
  • Your lesson progress (which chapters you've completed).
  • Your settings (theme, format, learning style).
  • Your display name, if you set one on My Path. It's only used to greet you, and only on that device.

Clearing your browser data deletes all of it. We have no copy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit, roughly where traffic comes from, and how long pages hold attention. This helps us figure out which lessons work. It uses cookies and collects standard technical data (page views, browser type, approximate region). It does not know your name, and we don't connect it to anything you do in the lessons.

If you block analytics with a browser extension or your browser's tracking protection, the whole site still works. Nothing breaks.

The one thing you can choose to send us

Some practice activities offer to log your practice minutes to our server so we can understand how the course is being used. That only happens if you opt in, and it sends just a first name and a minutes count. If you'd like yours removed, get in touch (see About) and tell us the name you used.

Data retention

Anything in your browser is yours and lives until you clear it. Opt-in practice logs on our server are kept while we're actively improving the course and deleted when they're no longer useful, or on request.

Lesson accounts

The interactive lessons use an optional account so your progress can follow you between devices. If you create one, here is exactly what we store on our server: your email and display name (to sign you in and greet you), which lessons and steps you've completed, your answers to lesson questions and the questionnaire, notes you choose to save, and roughly how many minutes you spend learning. We use it to save your progress and improve the lessons — nothing else. No ads, no selling, no spam.

Want your account and its data deleted? Reach out via the About page and we'll remove it.

Questions

If anything here is unclear or you want data removed, reach out via the About page.
