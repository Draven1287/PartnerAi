Privacy

What this site knows about you.

The honest version, in plain language. Short answer: very little, and none of it is for sale.

The promise

We will not sell your information. We will not send ads. We will not ask you for money. That's the deal, and everything below is just the detail.

What may stay in your browser

Learning AI uses localStorage — a small storage area inside your browser — to keep drafts and settings available on the device you are using. This can include:

  • An unfinished questionnaire draft, until you submit it.
  • Temporary lesson progress, including the free first lesson before you create an account.
  • Your settings (theme, format, learning style).

Clearing your browser data deletes this device copy. After you create an account, submitted progress and questionnaire answers are also stored with your Learning AI account as described below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit, including lesson-page routes, roughly where traffic comes from, and how long pages hold attention. This helps us figure out which lessons work. It uses cookies and collects standard technical data (page views, browser type, approximate region). It does not receive your Learning AI name, lesson answers, notes, or account progress.

If you block analytics with a browser extension or your browser's tracking protection, the whole site still works. Nothing breaks.

Optional learning-activity sharing

Learning-activity sharing is off unless you turn it on in Settings. If you opt in while signed in, Learning AI records active learning minutes with your account plus the current lesson or page path. This helps us understand which parts of the course hold attention. You can turn sharing off at any time; turning it off stops new activity records but does not delete records already saved.

Data retention

Browser copies may be removed when they successfully transfer into your account, when you sign out, when you choose to start the sample again, or when you clear browser data. Opt-in learning-activity records on our server are kept while we're actively improving the course and deleted when they're no longer useful, when you delete your Learning AI account, or on request.

Lesson accounts and the starting questionnaire

You can complete the first interactive lesson without an account. To save that lesson and open the rest of the course, Learning AI then requires an account and a six-category starting questionnaire. The questionnaire includes an age range—not a birth date—so the course can use age-appropriate guidance. Its other answers set a starting level, focus area, and lesson recommendation.

Here is what we store on our server: your email and display name (to sign you in and greet you), your selected age range, your six questionnaire answers and optional written details, which lessons and steps you complete, answers to lesson interactions, and notes you choose to save. We store active learning minutes only if you turn on learning-activity sharing in Settings. The questionnaire is required once during setup; you can retake it later. Learning AI administrators may access account and questionnaire records to operate, support, and improve the course. We do not use this information for ads, sell it, or send marketing spam.

If the account service is unavailable, you may still preview the first lesson, but you will not be able to create an account, submit the questionnaire, or unlock the full site until the service returns.

You can permanently delete your Learning AI account and its associated learning data from Settings. Type DELETE in the confirmation field; this cannot be undone. Deleting Learning AI data does not delete anything held by an outside AI service you used for practice.

For learners ages 13–17

Learning AI is designed for people aged 13 and older. If you are under 18, involve a parent, guardian, teacher, or another trusted adult when an activity takes you to an outside AI service—especially for school rules, private information, health, money, safety, or anything that feels uncomfortable.

This course does not contain a live AI chatbot. Some lessons invite you to practice in an outside AI tool. That outside service has its own terms and privacy rules, so check them before creating an account or sharing anything. Never paste passwords, account numbers, private photos, your home address, your school name, or another person's private information into an AI tool.
