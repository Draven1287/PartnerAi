V2 core lesson 02 · first win

Your first useful AI conversation.

Most people ask AI vague questions and get vague answers. This lesson gives you a useful win: a better learning conversation that protects your thinking.

Repair the prompt

The weak version

Help me understand this topic.

It is not wrong, but it gives the AI almost nothing to work with. Repair it by adding four pieces: topic, goal, current understanding, and what the AI should not do.

Weak tutor

"Here is the answer. Memorize these terms and you should be fine."

Fast, but it gives the answer away.

Better tutor

"First, explain the idea in your own words. I will give one hint, then quiz you before I show the full explanation."

Slower, but it protects learning.

The trap answer

Watch out for AI help that looks supportive but does not teach much:

"Do you understand now?"

That question is too vague. A better tutor asks a specific check: "What are the key parts, and how do they connect?"

First Useful AI Conversation

I am working on ________.
My goal is ________.
I already understand ________.
Help me by ________.
Do not ________ before I try.
Mastery check

Run the prompt once. Then write what changed after your follow-up. The win is not the first answer; the win is improving the conversation.
