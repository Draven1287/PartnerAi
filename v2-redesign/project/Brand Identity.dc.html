Brand identity · working draft

Learning AI

A free, interactive course that teaches people to use AI without letting it think for them — built by a high-schooler. The brand should feel the way the course does: warm, human, a little academic, and quietly premium. We'll lock each piece together — this is the starting point, not the final word.

04 · Voice

How it talks

In one line: warm · human · premium · academic · honest · clear.

Clear over clever
Short words, real examples. If a sentence needs re-reading, cut it.
Honest about AI
No hype. Name the limits. A confident answer can still be wrong.
Warm, not corporate
Say "you." Use contractions. Talk like a sharp friend, not a brand.
You stay in charge
Every lesson points back to the learner's judgment, never the tool's.
