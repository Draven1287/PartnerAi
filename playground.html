Click any prompt below to copy it. Then paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, DuckDuckGo AI Chat, Hugging Face Chat, or another AI tool. The point is to notice how better instructions create better answers.

How it works

Each prompt shows a different habit:

  1. Weak prompts show why vague requests get vague answers.
  2. Strong prompts add role, context, constraints, and a clear job.

You are not just trying to get an answer. You are learning how to steer the tool.
