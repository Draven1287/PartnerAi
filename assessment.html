First, map what you know and how you think about AI.

This is not about judging you. It checks two things at once: what AI concepts you understand and how you reason through real AI situations. You will answer one category at a time, then get a path built for your level and age range.

One important thing

You do not need to be scared of AI. You also should not worship it. The useful place is in the middle: curious, skeptical, and willing to learn how the system actually works.
