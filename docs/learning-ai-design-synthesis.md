# Learning AI — design synthesis

## Decision

Use the Open Design motion product proof as the leading product direction. Use the compact motion study as the interaction test bed. Claude Design has now produced a credible Phase 2 challenger, but it should contribute selected interaction and writing ideas rather than replace the Open Design direction.

The leading direction is not “luxury education.” It is **editorial control made interactive**: strong type, ruled spatial structure, dark proof moments used sparingly, learner-triggered transitions, visible decision ownership, and a 50-state record that grows through demonstrated capability.

## Final competitor decision

**Open Design remains the product lead.** It has the stronger overall information architecture, progress visualization, implementation mapping, and fit with the user's preferred direction. **Claude is the interaction-quality challenger.** Its strongest contributions are the concise learner thesis, the “commit before explanation” sample, the readable 10-arc journey, and the explicit capability/Decision Record completion language.

The production direction should therefore be a controlled synthesis, not a visual mash-up:

- Preserve Open Design's spatial grammar, progress system, routes, and restrained motion language.
- Adopt Claude's clearest learner-facing copy and its tested Chapter 25 gate behavior where these improve comprehension.
- Do not import Claude's page composition wholesale or create a second design system.
- Keep the Open Design color, spacing, type, and component decisions authoritative until the production token pass.

Claude's repaired Phase 2 proof was manually checked in Claude Design. The Frame choice now changes its helper to “Ready” and unlocks the next action; both required Practice gates pass; Saved Notes remains optional; the exit check blocks an incorrect answer and unlocks only on the correct answer; Prove and the Decision Record are reachable; text size cycles `A` → `A+` → `A++`; and reduced-motion and high-contrast controls expose active state. Claude's own QA reported and repaired a 4 px Craft-view overflow at 390 px, but that final narrow-width claim still needs independent browser verification after the file is exported.

## What the leading proof gets right

- Opens with the learner thesis: “Keep your judgment. Expand what you can do.”
- Uses one dominant decision per view instead of a dashboard full of statistics.
- Includes welcome/account, Today, Journey, all 50 real lessons, Decision Record, Access, Settings, implementation mapping, and critique routes.
- Maps existing lesson kinds into Frame → Understand → Practice → Capture → Prove without renaming stable chapter IDs.
- Requires substantive actions for progression while keeping Saved Notes optional.
- Keeps learner goals, AI contributions, checks, and decisions visibly separate.
- Makes Core 50 a permanent purchase and Continuum an optional future membership.
- Uses motion for entry, feedback, transfer, continuity, and completion—not ambience.
- Provides explicit and system reduced-motion paths.

## What must be improved before production

1. The account form and Settings controls still feel more generic than the lesson and Decision Record.
2. The display system depends on locally available Avenir Next; production needs a licensed/bundled or deliberate open-font choice.
3. The welcome headline needs a small left inset at wide viewports so its scale feels intentional rather than clipped.
4. The five lesson stages should remain understandable on mobile without exposing the full desktop rail at once.
5. Motion must be performance-tested on ordinary school laptops and mid-range phones.
6. The current proof demonstrates one complete lesson. The interaction family must be expanded across the other 18 lesson rhythms without turning them into one visual template.
7. Production authentication, syncing, payments, export, and cancellation still need real backend integration.
8. Live keyboard, screen-reader, Windows High Contrast, and reduced-motion sessions remain required before release.

## Product motion contract

| Moment | Movement | Learning purpose | Reduced-motion equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enter Today | Reading-order reveal, under 480 ms | Establish position, next action, and owned evidence | Immediate paint in the same reading order |
| Choose/predict | Selected object moves into the explanation | Preserve continuity between commitment and feedback | Selected state and explanation appear together |
| Reveal mechanism | Annotation layers enter locally | Show cause, evidence, or difference | Static annotated state |
| Record evidence | Learner text transfers into the Decision Record | Make authorship and ownership visible | Immediate record update with confirmation |
| Complete lesson | One state in the 50-field resolves | Connect demonstrated capability to progress | Static resolved state plus text announcement |
| Change arc | Scale or spatial relationship changes | Communicate a shift in perspective and difficulty | Clear heading/position change without translation |

## Curriculum-production sequence

Do not design all 50 lessons as 50 custom pages. Build a controlled library of interaction families, then compose each lesson from the family that best proves its capability.

1. Prediction and commitment
2. Classification and boundary setting
3. Annotated reveal
4. Before/after comparison
5. Prompt repair
6. Workflow sequencing
7. Independent verification
8. Permission and agent design
9. Live practice plus evidence capture
10. Transfer-oriented exit check

The authoritative lesson content remains in the chapter modules. The per-lesson capability, evidence, misconception, and transfer contract is in `docs/learning-ai-50-lesson-craft-dossier.md`.

## Acceptance gate for the next implementation pass

The product direction is ready to merge into the real V2 only when:

- all eight product areas render and route correctly;
- Chapter 7 completes end to end with two substantive gates plus `exitCheck`;
- notes never gate Next;
- mobile has no horizontal page overflow;
- reduced motion removes movement without removing information;
- keyboard focus remains visible and logical after every route/step transition;
- the 50 real lesson IDs and 10 arcs remain intact;
- the access page contains no purchase interruption inside a lesson;
- the lesson-quality audit remains at zero failures and zero warnings.
