# Learning AI — 50-lesson craft dossier

This dossier turns the existing 10-arc curriculum into a production contract. The lesson source files remain authoritative for titles, copy, activities, and stable `chapter-N` IDs. This file states what each lesson must help a learner do, what evidence the learner should leave with, the misconception it must unsettle, and where the capability should transfer.

## Course promise

The learner does not finish merely knowing more facts about AI. They finish able to frame a goal, understand what the system is doing, practice with checks, capture evidence, and prove what they can still decide and do themselves.

Every lesson uses **Frame → Understand → Practice → Capture → Prove**, contains at least two meaningful gated actions plus an `exitCheck`, and treats Saved Notes as optional reinforcement rather than a progression gate.

## Editorial definition of done

Before a lesson is declared finished:

1. The cold open requires a learner commitment before explanation.
2. The mechanism reveal is shorter than the practice and uses concrete examples.
3. At least two learner actions change the state of the lesson.
4. Feedback explains why, not merely right or wrong.
5. The captured evidence can be reused outside the lesson.
6. The `exitCheck` tests transfer to a fresh situation.
7. Completion names the capability earned without overclaiming mastery.
8. Keyboard, mobile, high-contrast, and reduced-motion paths preserve the task.
9. Saved Notes never gate Next.
10. The lesson still works if the learner chooses not to use an external AI tool.
